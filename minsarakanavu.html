Minsara Kanavu (1997) — Romance, Music & Dreams

Director & Co-writer: Rajiv Menon
Lead Cast: Arvind Swamy, Prabhu Deva, Kajol
Music: A. R. Rahman
Production: AVM Productions
Release Date: 14 January 1997
Genre: Musical romantic comedy
Recognition: Four National Film Awards

Minsara Kanavu is a 1997 Tamil musical romantic comedy directed and co-written by Rajiv Menon. It stars Arvind Swamy, Prabhu Deva, and Kajol, making it Kajol's Tamil film debut. A. R. Rahman composed the soundtrack.

Priya hopes to become a nun. Her childhood friend Thomas falls in love with her and asks Deva, a hairstylist known for changing people's minds, to help redirect her plans. As Deva gets to know Priya, his own feelings complicate the arrangement.

The film combines romance, friendship, and music. Songs such as "Vennilave" and "Strawberry Kanne" became popular highlights of Rahman's soundtrack.

Released during the Pongal season on 14 January 1997, Minsara Kanavu received several honours, including four National Film Awards.
